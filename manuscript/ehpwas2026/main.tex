\begin{abstract}
Hospital federated learning (FL) must simultaneously resist gradient inversion, Byzantine poisoning, and Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) risk under multi-year retention. \textbf{ZKFL-PQ} composes NIST ML-KEM-768 transport; a dual-norm gate ($\ell_2$ Unruh NIZK in $\mathcal{L}_{\tau}^{\mathrm{bind}}$ plus public $\ell_\infty$ clipping on the same quantized vector); \emph{Unruh-lifted} Enc-consistency for BFV coins; full-vector BFV opened by $(t,n)$-threshold partial decryption with a PartialDecrypt NIZK (malicious $\mu_i$ abort); round-transcript binding and adaptive public $\tau$; and default post-ZKP median. The artifact ships fused SEAL+threshold HE, shared-SIS Unruh, Keccak/SHA3 formal checks, and an innovation-pack evaluation. Empirically we report multi-seed baselines, UCI Breast Cancer under the innovation stack, MedMNIST/\texttt{ConvNet28}, backdoor ASR, and a sparse-poison dual-norm ablation ($100\%$ reject vs.\ $0\%$ for $\ell_2$-only). Complementary to Beskar-class secure aggregation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Federated learning (FL)~\cite{mcmahan2017fedavg} keeps raw records at the edge, yet model updates remain vulnerable to gradient inversion~\cite{zhu2019deep}, Byzantine poisoning~\cite{blanchard2017machine}, and HNDL attacks against classical TLS when medical archives outlive RSA/ECC~\cite{mosca2018cybersecurity}. Hospital consortia therefore need (i)~post-quantum (PQ) session confidentiality, (ii)~verifiable rejection of oversized updates, and (iii)~aggregation that does not place a single curious server in sole possession of plaintext gradients.

Clinical deployments retain data for years to decades, operate under multi-institutional governance (no single root of trust), and face regulators that ask how updates are authenticated and who can decrypt them. Wrapping FedAvg in classical TLS is insufficient against adversaries that store ciphertexts today and open them later~\cite{mosca2018cybersecurity}. Cryptographic accept alone is also insufficient: updates whose $\ell_2$ norm respects a public threshold $\tau$ can still flip signs or implant triggers, so statistical robust aggregation must follow a successful proof.

\paragraph{Design goals.}
We optimize for hospital consortia where (G1)~ciphertext retention is measured in years, (G2)~no single IT operator may hold a usable $sk$, (G3)~oversized gradients must be rejectable without trusting the client, and (G4)~in-bound Byzantine behavior that passes $\tau$ is handled by a robust aggregator rather than by tightening $\tau$ until honest sites fail. These goals motivate the four-layer stack rather than any single primitive.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item A layered protocol that binds Unruh norm proofs to BFV ciphertexts via $\mathcal{L}_{\tau}^{\mathrm{bind}}$ + \emph{Unruh} Enc-consistency, opens with threshold partial decrypt \emph{with} PartialDecrypt NIZK, then robust-aggregates (default median), with each layer addressing a distinct threat (Table~\ref{tab:threats}).
  \item Dual-norm gating ($\ell_2$ Unruh + public $\ell_\infty$ clip), adaptive public $\tau$, and round-transcript binding---closing QROM-uniformity, sparse-poison, decryptor-malice, and cross-round swap gaps in prior residuals.
  \item Concrete parameters, a named soundness/privacy theorem, Lean/Python/\texttt{QROM.ec} checks with SHA3/Keccak lane lemmas, and measured payloads under varying $r$ and HE degree.
  \item A reproducible artifact~\cite{zkflcode} and empirical study: multi-seed baselines, innovation-pack UCI run, MedMNIST/\texttt{ConvNet28}, backdoor ASR, and dual-norm sparse-poison ablation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Threat Model and Claims}
\textbf{Adversary:} (i)~a network eavesdropper with future quantum computers against factoring/DLP; (ii)~up to $f{<}N/2$ Byzantine clients that may submit arbitrary vectors and proofs; (iii)~a curious aggregator that sees all protocol messages but does not hold $sk$ alone; (iv)~up to $t{-}1$ colluding threshold decryptors, \emph{plus} the possibility that one decryptor returns a malformed partial $\mu_i$.

\textbf{Trust.} Honest clients follow local SGD, $\ell_\infty$ clipping, and the Prove interface. Honest decryptors compute $\mu_i$ correctly (malicious partials are caught by PartialDecrypt NIZK and abort the open). The aggregator correctly runs $\Verify$ and the chosen robust aggregator.

\textbf{Claims:} ML-KEM-768 transport (FIPS~203 Level~3)~\cite{nist_fips203_2024}; dual-norm soundness for oversized / sparse witnesses under SIS + Unruh/RO; Unruh Enc-consistency so a proof cannot attach to a mismatched $\mathbf{ct}$; threshold privacy \emph{and} open correctness against a malicious partial; transcript non-swap across rounds.

\textbf{Scope.} We do not claim differential privacy or a drop-in replacement for Beskar-class full-stack secure aggregation~\cite{zhang2025beskar}. ZKFL-PQ targets verifiable norm gating, multi-party decrypt, and robust post-filtering; pure $\ell_2$ alone does not stop sign-flip or in-bound backdoors (Table~\ref{tab:threats}).

\section{Protocol Design}

\subsection{Round Structure}
Each round $t$: the server broadcasts $w^{(t)}$. Client $i$ runs local SGD on $D_i$, quantizes to $\tilde{w}_i$, encrypts under BFV public key $pk$, proves $\norm{\tilde{w}_i}_2\le\tau$ bound to $\mathbf{ct}_i$ (and Enc-consistency of coins), and sends an ML-KEM-wrapped payload. The server verifies proofs, threshold-opens each accepted ciphertext, and applies coordinate-wise median (default) before the model step (Algorithm~\ref{alg:target}).

\paragraph{Transport.}
ML-KEM-768 encapsulates a session key used to AES-CTR-wrap $(\mathbf{ct}_i,\pi_i)$. This protects in-transit confidentiality against classical and future quantum adversaries on the channel; it does not replace HE confidentiality against the aggregator, which follows from RLWE + threshold opening.

\paragraph{Verification checklist.}
$\Verify(\pi_i,\mathbf{ct}_i,\tau)$ returns $1$ iff: (i)~Unruh invertible-RO records are consistent; (ii)~Fiat--Shamir/Unruh challenge bits match the recomputed digest of first messages and $\mathbf{ct}_i$; (iii)~each session satisfies the SIS algebraic relation against the shared $\mathbf{A}$; (iv)~response norms respect the rejection bound $B$; (v)~Enc-consistency accepts for every BFV chunk. Fail-closed: any failed check drops the client for that round.

\subsection{Quantization and Dual-Norm Gate}
$\mathrm{Quantize}$ clips coordinates to $[-c,c]$, scales by $s$, and rounds into $\mathbb{Z}_t$ (artifact defaults $c{=}1$, $s{=}10^{3}$). Before prove+encrypt, honest clients apply a public $\ell_\infty$ clip $\tilde{w}\leftarrow\mathrm{Clip}_\infty(\Delta,\tau_\infty)$ and then prove $\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\le\tau_2$ on the \emph{same} vector that is encrypted. Sparse spikes with $\norm{\cdot}_2\le\tau_2$ but $\norm{\cdot}_\infty>\tau_\infty$ are rejected before opening; Enc-consistency prevents proving a clipped vector while encrypting an unclipped one. Adaptive public $\tau_2$ is updated from accepted-norm quantiles and bound into the next round's associated data together with a rolling transcript hash $H(\mathsf{prev})$.

\subsection{Bound Norm Language}
\begin{definition}[Bound language]
Let $C=\Commit(\tilde{w};\mathbf{r})=\mathbf{A}(\tilde{w}\|\mathbf{r})\bmod q'$
and $\mathbf{ct}=\Enc_{pk}(\tilde{w};\rho)$. Then
\begin{multline}
\mathcal{L}_{\tau}^{\mathrm{bind}}=
\bigl\{(C,\mathbf{ct},\tau):\exists(\tilde{w},\mathbf{r},\rho)\\
\quad C=\Commit(\tilde{w};\mathbf{r}),\
\mathbf{ct}=\Enc(\tilde{w};\rho),\
\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\le\tau\bigr\}.
\end{multline}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{$\Sigma$-protocol~\cite{lyubashevsky2012lattice}.}
Commit masks $\mathbf{y}$ and message $\tilde{w}$; the Fiat--Shamir challenge is $e=H(C\|\mathbf{ct}\|T\|\tau)$; responses $(\mathbf{z},\mathbf{r}_z)$ satisfy $T+eC\equiv\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{z}\|\mathbf{r}_z)$ and $\norm{\mathbf{z}}_2\le B$. Rejection sampling yields HVZK in the classical ROM. Acceptance uses only algebraic and norm checks---never a client-supplied Boolean.

\paragraph{Unruh-oriented NIZK.}
Classical FS is not tightly QROM-secure~\cite{kiltz2018qrom}. We use parallel binary Unruh sessions (library default $r{=}128$; latency-sensitive demos often $4$--$64$) with invertible-RO records $(\rho,H(\rho))$. Challenge bits are extracted from $H(\mathsf{UNRUH}\|\tau\|\mathbf{ct}\|\mathsf{first\text{-}msgs})$. All sessions share one SIS matrix $\mathbf{A}$ (not $r$ independent keys), which keeps prove/verify interactive at imaging-scale $d$ (one $256{\times}(d{+}128)$ matvec family instead of $r$ separate keys). Combinatorial uniqueness / $2^{-r}$ lemmas are machine-checked in Lean~4; executable game hops and a SHA3-instantiated EasyCrypt QROM library with bit-level Keccak-$f$[1600] lane lemmas (θ-column parity, ρ/π/χ/ι locality, packing invertibility, 24-round fold) accompany the artifact (\texttt{formal/run\_formal\_ci.py}).

\paragraph{Enc-consistency (Unruh-lifted).}
Beyond hashing $\mathbf{ct}$ into the challenge, a dedicated Σ-gadget proves knowledge of coins $\rho=(u,e_0,e_1)$ such that the BFV encryption equations hold for the same plaintext chunk. We lift this gadget under the same Unruh transform as the norm proof (parallel binary sessions + invertible RO records), closing the QROM-uniformity residual between the two ct-bound proofs. Enc-consistency first messages are absorbed into the norm proof's associated data.

Additive BFV yields $\Dec(\sum_i\Enc(m_i))=\sum_i m_i$ under a noise budget growing roughly as $N\cdot O(\sigma\sqrt{n})$. Full-vector packing encrypts all $d$ coordinates as $\lceil d/n\rceil$ independent ciphertexts under the same $pk$; the ZKP covers the concatenated quantized vector, so partial packing cannot hide an unprotected suffix. After key generation, secret coefficients are Shamir-shared and the monolithic $sk$ is erased. Opening uses Lagrange-weighted partial decryptions with smudging: party $i$ returns $\mu_i=c_1\star(\lambda_i\cdot s_i)$ (plus noise). Each partial is accompanied by a PartialDecrypt NIZK proving knowledge of $s_{\mathrm{eff}}$ with $\mu_i=c_1\star s_{\mathrm{eff}}$; a tampered $\mu_i$ fails verify and aborts the open---extending threshold privacy to \emph{open correctness}. Modular negacyclic multiplication with parallel per-chunk encryption keeps full-vector runs interactive on CPU for $d{\approx}5{\times}10^{4}$. An optional TenSEAL/SEAL sidecar runs as \texttt{FusedSealThresholdHE}.

\paragraph{Post-ZKP aggregation.}
Let $S$ be the set of clients that pass $\Verify$. Default aggregation is the coordinate-wise median $\bar{\Delta}_j=\mathrm{med}\{\Delta_{i,j}:i\in S\}$. Multi-Krum and plaintext mean are available as overrides. Median is intentionally applied \emph{after} cryptographic accept: it does not replace the ZKP, and the ZKP does not replace median.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{ZKFL-PQ round (default composition)}
\label{alg:target}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require $w^{(t)}$, $\tau$, KEM $(ek_S,dk_S)$, HE $pk$, decryptor shares
\State Broadcast $w^{(t)}$
\For{each client $i$}
  \State $\tilde{w}\gets\mathrm{Quantize}(\mathrm{LocalSGD}(w^{(t)},D_i))$
  \State $\mathbf{ct}_i\gets\Enc_{pk}(\tilde{w};\rho_i)$; prove Enc-consistency of $\rho_i$
  \State $\pi_i\gets\Prove_{\mathrm{Unruh}}((\mathbf{ct}_i,\tau),\tilde{w})$
  \State send ML-KEM-wrapped $(\mathbf{ct}_i,\pi_i)$
\EndFor
\State Keep $i$ iff cryptographic verify returns $1$
\State For each accepted $i$: threshold-open $\mathbf{ct}_i$ to recover $\Delta_i$
\State $\bar{\Delta}\gets\mathrm{Median}(\{\Delta_i\})$ \Comment{or Multi-Krum; not plaintext mean alone}
\State $w^{(t+1)}\gets w^{(t)}+\bar{\Delta}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{theorem}[Composition soundness and threshold privacy]
Assume SIS binding of $\Commit$, RLWE semantic security of BFV, and RO modeling of $H{=}\mathsf{SHA3\text{-}256}$. Then: (i)~honest dual-norm witnesses are accepted except for rejection-sampling aborts; (ii)~an oversized or $\ell_\infty$-violating witness is rejected except with Unruh/SIS advantage; (iii)~Unruh Enc-consistency plus digesting $\mathbf{ct}$ prevent attaching a valid proof to a mismatched ciphertext; (iv)~the aggregator and any $t{-}1$ decryptors learn nothing beyond what $t$ partial opens reveal, and a single malformed $\mu_i$ is rejected by PartialDecrypt NIZK (open aborts); (v)~round transcripts bind across rounds via $H(\mathsf{prev})$. Combinatorial Unruh uniqueness/$2^{-r}$ is machine-checked in Lean~4; EasyCrypt theories instantiate $H$ as SHA3-256 with Keccak lane lemmas (\texttt{formal/run\_formal\_ci.py}).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Implementation invariants.}
The public repository~\cite{zkflcode} realizes Algorithm~\ref{alg:target} with: (i)~identical prove/encrypt dimension $d$; (ii)~ciphertext bytes in Unruh challenges; (iii)~crypto-only accept (no trusted Boolean); (iv)~full-vector packing; (v)~Enc-consistency; (vi)~fused SEAL+threshold HE; (vii)~default median; (viii)~one shared SIS matrix per Unruh prover. Scripts pin seeds and emit JSON under \texttt{results/}.

\subsection{Scale, Medical, and Backdoor Studies}
A compact-model scale study with $N{=}20$ clients and $T{=}30$ rounds reproduces the same qualitative pattern under both attacks: ZKP and hybrid reject oversized updates with high probability, while sign-flip still requires a robust aggregator. Wall-clock remains interactive on CPU for the compact MLP used in that study, which is the intended operating point for validating the crypto pipeline before scaling encoders.

On UCI Breast Cancer Wisconsin (569 samples, 30 clinical features; compact MLP with $d{=}1554$), the innovation stack---Unruh $\ell_2$ ($r{=}16$ in the pack demo; library default $128$), Unruh Enc-consistency ($r_{\mathrm{enc}}{=}4$), PartialDecrypt NIZK, adaptive $\tau_2$, transcript binding, $\ell_\infty$ clip, full-vector threshold HE, and post-ZKP median---yields accuracies $89.5\%\!\rightarrow\!93.0\%\!\rightarrow\!92.1\%$ while rejecting the oversized client on the attack round; public $\tau_2$ adapts $8\!\rightarrow\!4.3\!\rightarrow\!2.3\!\rightarrow\!2.0$ (\texttt{results/innovation\_pack\_results.json}). Mean round time is $\approx 20$--$23$\,s on a laptop CPU for this denser proof surface. A sparse-poison ablation ($\norm{\cdot}_2\le\tau_2$ but $\norm{\cdot}_\infty>\tau_\infty$) shows dual-norm \textbf{100\%} reject vs.\ \textbf{0\%} for $\ell_2$-only, with $\approx$265$\times$ lower post-aggregation spike on the poisoned coordinate. Full-resolution PneumoniaMNIST /\texttt{ConvNet28} results from Sec.~V-B remain complementary imaging smoke tests.

Trigger backdoors whose poisoned updates remain inside $(\tau_2,\tau_\infty)$ are still only partially mitigated by median (ASR $\approx$55\% vs.\ $\approx$98\% for FedAvg/$\ell_2$; Fig.~\ref{fig:bd}); dual-norm removes sparse spikes \emph{before} opening, while median remains necessary for diffuse in-bound poisons.

\section{Security Discussion}
Honest clients with $\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\le\tau$ produce Unruh sessions that pass algebraic and norm checks except for rejection-sampling aborts (bounded retries in the artifact). If $\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\gg\tau$, sessions with challenge bit $1$ fail with overwhelming probability under the Gaussian mask design; soundness therefore depends on the fraction of $1$-challenges across $r$ repetitions. The library default $r{=}128$ targets a $\approx 128$-bit combinatorial Unruh class; demos may reduce $r$ for CPU latency at linear proof-size cost. Binding $H$ to ciphertext bytes, together with the Enc-consistency gadget for $\rho$, prevents a benign proof from being attached to a different ciphertext. BFV semantic security (RLWE) hides individual updates from parties lacking $sk$; with Shamir $(t,n)$, the aggregator and any $t{-}1$ decryptors cannot open. We do not claim differential privacy; sites that need DP can compose mechanisms as in Beskar~\cite{zhang2025beskar}.

\paragraph{Attack games (informal).}
\emph{Oversized/sparse:} adversary wins if $\Verify=1$ on a witness violating $(\tau_2,\tau_\infty)$. \emph{Split:} proof for $\mathbf{ct}$ verifies against $\mathbf{ct}'\neq\mathbf{ct}$. \emph{Malicious partial:} forged $\mu_i$ is accepted. \emph{Curious open:} aggregator + ${<}t$ decryptors recover an honest plaintext. Theorem~1 bounds these under SIS/Unruh/RO, PartialDecrypt NIZK, and RLWE+Shamir.

\paragraph{Layering and deployment.}
Table~\ref{tab:threats} is the scientific punchline: no single primitive meets the joint medical threat model. In a hospital consortium, each site runs a client; a neutral coordinator verifies Unruh norm + Unruh Enc-consistency + dual-norm checks; $t$-of-$n$ share holders open with PartialDecrypt NIZK (abort on malice); median yields the round update; adaptive $\tau_2$ and the transcript hash are published for the next round. Daily/weekly training absorbs the denser proof surface ($\approx$20\,s/round on UCI in the innovation pack). Imaging-scale $d$ remains interactive for Unruh+HE when SIS keys are shared and BFV mul is modular; local SGD still dominates \texttt{ConvNet28} wall-clock.

\section{Limitations and Reproducibility}
Bit-level Keccak-$f$[1600] lane lemmas are EasyCrypt-axiomatized with a FIPS executable checker; full discharge without the Python oracle remains future work. Unruh Enc-consistency multiplies Enc prove cost by $r_{\mathrm{enc}}$; demos use small $r_{\mathrm{enc}}$ for CPU budgets while the library default keeps large $r$ for the norm proof. PartialDecrypt NIZK is a research-stack FS proof of the linear relation $\mu=c_1\star s_{\mathrm{eff}}$ (not MPC-in-the-head). Demo HE uses $n{=}512$; $n{=}4096$ is available via \texttt{ZKFL\_HE\_PRESET}. Median+dual-norm reduce but do not eliminate diffuse backdoors---Beskar-style DP remains complementary. Reproduce via \texttt{python experiments/run\_innovation\_pack.py}, \texttt{run\_target\_protocol.py}, and \texttt{formal/run\_formal\_ci.py}.

\section{Conclusion}
ZKFL-PQ advances quantum-resilient medical FL by making the ct-bound stack QROM-uniform (Unruh Enc-consistency), decryptor-accountable (PartialDecrypt NIZK), dual-norm gated, transcript-bound, and adaptively thresholded---then composing with threshold BFV and median. Empirically, sparse $\ell_\infty$ spikes are rejected cryptographically ($100\%$ vs.\ $0\%$ under $\ell_2$-only), oversized clients are still caught, and UCI accuracy remains competitive under the denser proof surface ($89.5\%\!\rightarrow\!93.0\%\!\rightarrow\!92.1\%$). The artifact is complementary to Beskar-class secure aggregation. Artifact: \url{https://github.com/edlansiaux/pq-zkfl-medical}.
