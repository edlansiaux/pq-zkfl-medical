\begin{abstract}
Hospital FL faces gradient inversion, Byzantine poisoning, and Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) risk under multi-year retention. \textbf{ZKFL-PQ}'s contribution is a \emph{defense-in-depth composition}: NIST ML-KEM-768 transport; an Unruh NIZK (default $r{=}128$) of $\ell_2$-bounded updates in language $\mathcal{L}_{\tau}^{\mathrm{bind}}$ with an Enc-consistency gadget for BFV coins; full-vector BFV aggregation opened by $(t,n)$-threshold \emph{partial} decryption ($sk$ never reconstructed); and a default post-ZKP coordinate-wise median that closes sign-flip/backdoor residuals that pure $\ell_2$ gates must accept. A public artifact ships fused SEAL+threshold HE, Lean/Python/EasyCrypt combinatorial Unruh checks, and WiMob-oriented payload knobs. Empirically: multi-seed synthetic baselines, scale $N{=}20$/$T{=}30$, UCI Breast Cancer, full-resolution PneumoniaMNIST, and a backdoor ASR win for ZKP+median vs.\ FedAvg/$\ell_2$-only. We complement---not replace---Beskar-class secure aggregation.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
Post-quantum cryptography, federated learning, zero-knowledge proofs, homomorphic encryption, medical AI, Byzantine robustness
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}
Federated learning (FL)~\cite{mcmahan2017fedavg} keeps raw records at the edge, yet model updates remain vulnerable to gradient inversion~\cite{zhu2019deep}, Byzantine poisoning~\cite{blanchard2017machine}, and HNDL attacks against classical TLS when medical archives outlive RSA/ECC~\cite{mosca2018cybersecurity}. Hospital consortia therefore need (i)~post-quantum (PQ) session confidentiality, (ii)~verifiable rejection of oversized updates, and (iii)~aggregation that does not place a single curious server in sole possession of plaintext gradients.

Clinical deployments retain data for years to decades, operate under multi-institutional governance (no single root of trust), and face regulators that ask how updates are authenticated and who can decrypt them. Wrapping FedAvg in classical TLS is insufficient against adversaries that store ciphertexts today and open them later~\cite{mosca2018cybersecurity}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item \textbf{Composition punchline:} bind Unruh norm proofs to BFV ciphertexts via $\mathcal{L}_{\tau}^{\mathrm{bind}}$ + Enc-consistency, open with threshold partial decrypt, then robust-aggregate (default median)---each layer covers a distinct threat (Table~\ref{tab:threats}).
  \item Concrete Unruh ($r{=}128$), BFV presets, and WiMob payload knobs ($r$, HE degree) with a named soundness/privacy theorem supported by Lean/Python/\texttt{QROM.ec} combinatorial checks.
  \item Reproducible artifact~\cite{zkflcode}: full-vector encrypt+prove, crypto-only accept, fused SEAL+threshold path, no monolithic server $sk$.
  \item Empirics that make the niche sharp: large-norm vs.\ sign-flip honesty; backdoor ASR cut by ZKP+median (Fig.~\ref{fig:bd}); UCI + full-res MedMNIST; scale $N{=}20$, $T{=}30$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Threat Model and Claims}
\textbf{Adversary:} (i)~network eavesdropper with future quantum computers against factoring/DLP; (ii)~up to $f{<}N/2$ Byzantine clients; (iii)~curious aggregator that sees protocol messages but does not hold $sk$ alone; (iv)~up to $t{-}1$ colluding threshold decryptors.

\textbf{Claims:} ML-KEM-768 transport (FIPS~203 Level~3)~\cite{nist_fips203_2024}; norm soundness for oversized witnesses under SIS + (Unruh/)RO assumptions; ciphertext binding so a proof cannot be replayed on a different $\mathbf{ct}$; aggregator privacy via Shamir-shared $sk$ and partial decryption $\mu_i=c_1\star(\lambda_i\cdot s_i)$.

\textbf{Scope.} We do not claim differential privacy or a drop-in replacement for Beskar-class full-stack secure aggregation~\cite{zhang2025beskar}. ZKFL-PQ is the complementary \emph{norm gate + multi-hospital decrypt + robust post-filter} stack; pure $\ell_2$ alone does not stop sign-flip or in-bound backdoors (Table~\ref{tab:threats}).

\section{Protocol Design}

\subsection{Round Structure}
Each round $t$: the server broadcasts $w^{(t)}$. Client $i$ runs local SGD on $D_i$, quantizes to $\tilde{w}_i$, encrypts under BFV public key $pk$, proves $\norm{\tilde{w}_i}_2\le\tau$ bound to $\mathbf{ct}_i$ (and Enc-consistency of coins), and sends an ML-KEM-wrapped payload. The server verifies proofs, threshold-opens each accepted ciphertext, and applies coordinate-wise median (default) before the model step (Algorithm~\ref{alg:target}).

\subsection{Quantization}
$\mathrm{Quantize}$ clips coordinates to $[-c,c]$, scales by $s$, and rounds into $\mathbb{Z}_t$ (artifact defaults $c{=}1$, $s{=}10^{3}$). The public threshold $\tau$ applies after quantization; adaptive rules $\tau^{(t)}=\hat\mu{+}k\hat\sigma$ are compatible. Reported runs use fixed $\tau$ (synthetic $5$; medical $8$--$12$).

\subsection{Bound Norm Language}
\begin{definition}[Bound language]
Let $C=\Commit(\tilde{w};\mathbf{r})=\mathbf{A}(\tilde{w}\|\mathbf{r})\bmod q'$
and $\mathbf{ct}=\Enc_{pk}(\tilde{w};\rho)$. Then
\begin{multline}
\mathcal{L}_{\tau}^{\mathrm{bind}}=
\bigl\{(C,\mathbf{ct},\tau):\exists(\tilde{w},\mathbf{r},\rho)\\
\quad C=\Commit(\tilde{w};\mathbf{r}),\
\mathbf{ct}=\Enc(\tilde{w};\rho),\
\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\le\tau\bigr\}.
\end{multline}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{$\Sigma$-protocol~\cite{lyubashevsky2012lattice}.}
Commit masks $\mathbf{y}$ and message $\tilde{w}$; the Fiat--Shamir challenge is $e=H(C\|\mathbf{ct}\|T\|\tau)$; responses $(\mathbf{z},\mathbf{r}_z)$ satisfy $T+eC\equiv\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{z}\|\mathbf{r}_z)$ and $\norm{\mathbf{z}}_2\le B$. Rejection sampling yields HVZK in the classical ROM.

\paragraph{Unruh-oriented NIZK.}
Classical FS is not tightly QROM-secure~\cite{kiltz2018qrom}. We use parallel binary Unruh sessions (library default $r{=}128$; latency-sensitive demos often $32$--$64$) with invertible-RO records. Combinatorial uniqueness / $2^{-r}$ lemmas are machine-checked in Lean~4; executable game hops and an in-repository EasyCrypt QROM interface (\texttt{formal/easycrypt/QROM.ec}) accompany the artifact.

\paragraph{Enc-consistency.}
Beyond hashing $\mathbf{ct}$ into the challenge, a dedicated Σ-gadget proves knowledge of coins $\rho=(u,e_0,e_1)$ such that the BFV encryption equations hold for the same plaintext chunk, closing split attacks that pair a benign proof with a mismatched ciphertext.

Additive BFV yields $\Dec(\sum_i\Enc(m_i))=\sum_i m_i$ under a noise budget growing roughly as $N\cdot O(\sigma\sqrt{n})$. After key generation, secret coefficients are Shamir-shared and the monolithic $sk$ is erased. Opening uses Lagrange-weighted partial decryptions with smudging: $s$ is never reconstructed for production decrypt. An optional TenSEAL/SEAL sidecar runs in the same process as threshold BFV (\texttt{FusedSealThresholdHE}): primary open remains partial-decrypt, while SEAL provides a certified encoder path and consistency check.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{ZKFL-PQ round (default composition)}
\label{alg:target}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require $w^{(t)}$, $\tau$, KEM $(ek_S,dk_S)$, HE $pk$, decryptor shares
\State Broadcast $w^{(t)}$
\For{each client $i$}
  \State $\tilde{w}\gets\mathrm{Quantize}(\mathrm{LocalSGD}(w^{(t)},D_i))$
  \State $\mathbf{ct}_i\gets\Enc_{pk}(\tilde{w};\rho_i)$; prove Enc-consistency of $\rho_i$
  \State $\pi_i\gets\Prove_{\mathrm{Unruh}}((\mathbf{ct}_i,\tau),\tilde{w})$
  \State send ML-KEM-wrapped $(\mathbf{ct}_i,\pi_i)$
\EndFor
\State Keep $i$ iff cryptographic verify returns $1$
\State For each accepted $i$: threshold-open $\mathbf{ct}_i$ to recover $\Delta_i$
\State $\bar{\Delta}\gets\mathrm{Median}(\{\Delta_i\})$ \Comment{or Multi-Krum; not plaintext mean alone}
\State $w^{(t+1)}\gets w^{(t)}+\bar{\Delta}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{theorem}[Composition soundness and threshold privacy]
Assume SIS binding of $\Commit$, RLWE semantic security of BFV, and RO modeling of $H$. Then: (i)~honest $\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\le\tau$ proofs are accepted except for rejection-sampling aborts; (ii)~an oversized witness is accepted with probability at most $\approx 2^{-r}$ (binary Unruh) plus SIS advantage; (iii)~Enc-consistency plus digesting $\mathbf{ct}$ into challenges prevent attaching a valid proof to a mismatched ciphertext; (iv)~the aggregator and any $t{-}1$ decryptors learn nothing beyond what $t$ partial opens reveal. Combinatorial Unruh uniqueness/$2^{-r}$ is machine-checked in Lean~4 with executable Python game hops and in-repo EasyCrypt \texttt{QROM.ec} (no claim of a full SHA3-QROM production proof).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Implementation.}
The public repository~\cite{zkflcode} realizes Algorithm~\ref{alg:target}: shared prove/encrypt dimension, ciphertext-bound challenges, crypto-only accept, full-vector packing, Enc-consistency, fused SEAL+threshold HE, and default median. Scripts pin seeds and emit JSON.

\subsection{Scale, Medical, and Backdoor Studies}
A compact-model scale study with $N{=}20$ clients and $T{=}30$ rounds reproduces the same qualitative pattern under both attacks: ZKP and hybrid reject oversized updates with high probability, while sign-flip still requires a robust aggregator. Wall-clock remains interactive on CPU for the compact MLP used in that study, which is the intended operating point for validating the crypto pipeline before scaling encoders.

On UCI Breast Cancer Wisconsin (569 samples, 30 clinical features; compact MLP with $d{=}1554$), the target stack---Unruh with $r{=}64$ (library default $128$), full-vector fused HE, $(2,3)$ partial threshold decrypt, and post-ZKP median---yields accuracies $88.6\%\!\rightarrow\!93.0\%\!\rightarrow\!\mathbf{93.9\%}$ across three rounds while rejecting a scripted oversized client (Fig.~\ref{fig:med}). Mean round time is $\approx 4$--$9$\,s and per-round payload $\approx 1.4$--$1.7$\,MB on a laptop CPU. Full-resolution PneumoniaMNIST uses native $28\times 28$ images ($784$ pixels, no learned or random projection), a compact head, and $25$ HE chunks; accuracy reaches about $67.5\%$ by round three under the same cryptographic stack, with oversized updates rejected.

Trigger backdoors whose poisoned updates remain inside $\tau$ are accepted by pure $\ell_2$ ZKPs (ASR $\approx$98\%, matching FedAvg). Default ZKP+median cuts ASR to $\approx$55\% while preserving clean accuracy $\approx$99\%; Multi-Krum alone reaches $\approx$46\% ASR (Fig.~\ref{fig:bd}). The win story is composition: cryptographic accept for oversized updates, statistical robust aggregation for in-bound poisons.

\section{Security Discussion}
Honest clients with $\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\le\tau$ produce Unruh sessions that pass algebraic and norm checks except for rejection-sampling aborts (bounded retries in the artifact). If $\norm{\tilde{w}}_2\gg\tau$, sessions with challenge bit $1$ fail with overwhelming probability under the Gaussian mask design; soundness therefore depends on the fraction of $1$-challenges across $r$ repetitions. The library default $r{=}128$ targets a $\approx 128$-bit combinatorial Unruh class; demos may reduce $r$ for CPU latency at linear proof-size cost. Binding $H$ to ciphertext bytes, together with the Enc-consistency gadget for $\rho$, prevents a benign proof from being attached to a different ciphertext. BFV semantic security (RLWE) hides individual updates from parties lacking $sk$; with Shamir $(t,n)$, the aggregator and any $t{-}1$ decryptors cannot open. We do not claim differential privacy; sites that need DP can compose mechanisms as in Beskar~\cite{zhang2025beskar}.

\paragraph{Layering.}
Table~\ref{tab:threats} summarizes the division of labor: ML-KEM for PQ transit, ZKP+binding+Enc-consistency for oversized and split attacks, threshold open for curious aggregators, median for in-bound Byzantine. None of the layers alone meets the joint medical threat model; the contribution is their composition with explicit binding.

\paragraph{Deployment.}
In a hospital consortium, each site runs a client; a neutral coordinator verifies proofs; $t$-of-$n$ share holders (for example a DPO and two hospital CISOs) jointly open per-client ciphertexts; median aggregation yields the round update. Daily or weekly training absorbs the observed $\sim$20$\times$ CPU overhead vs.\ plaintext FedAvg (Fig.~\ref{fig:break}). For imaging-scale $d$, HE chunks and Unruh proofs grow with $d$; tabular/compact models (UCI) validate the crypto pipeline first.

\section{Limitations}
Research-grade residuals remain: a full SHA3 instantiation inside a production EasyCrypt QROM library, and larger imaging CNNs without compact heads. The default artifact already delivers binding, full-vector HE, threshold privacy, fused SEAL+threshold, and post-ZKP median.

\section{Conclusion}
ZKFL-PQ's punchline is defense-in-depth for medical FL: ML-KEM-768, ciphertext-bound Unruh+Enc-consistency, threshold BFV partial open, and default median---with honest measurement where $\ell_2$ alone fails. Artifact: \url{https://github.com/edlansiaux/pq-zkfl-medical}.
